# Supplier Onboarding and Vendor Master Checklist SO-050 (Synthetic)

Document ID: SO-050. Version 2.0. Owner: Vendor Master Data. Synthetic document for the ChainSleuth demonstration.

## Identity and Ownership Verification

Before a supplier record is activated, the onboarding team verifies the legal entity, its registered owners and its controlling persons. Where two supplier records report the same controlling owner, the onboarding team must document the business reason for maintaining separate records.

## Bank Account Verification

Each payout bank account is verified by a call-back to a known contact at the supplier. A bank account that is already registered to another supplier record triggers a mandatory secondary review. Accounts belonging to registered payment intermediaries, such as approved factoring agents, are recorded with an intermediary flag and are exempt from the secondary review.

## Periodic Review

Supplier records with shared payout accounts, shared owners, or similar names are reviewed at least quarterly. Findings from these reviews are recorded against the supplier record together with the reviewer's decision.

# Procurement Policy PP-100 (Synthetic)

Document ID: PP-100. Version 3.2. Owner: Procurement Controls Office. This is a synthetic document created for the ChainSleuth demonstration.

## Three-Way Match Requirement

Every supplier invoice must be matched to an approved purchase order and to goods-receipt evidence before payment approval. The match is performed per product line, comparing the billed quantity, the ordered quantity and the quantity recorded as delivered. Invoices that cannot be linked to a purchase order must be routed to the Accounts Payable exceptions queue and must not be approved for payment until the link is resolved or a documented exemption is recorded.

## Quantity Tolerance

A billed quantity may exceed the delivered quantity by no more than 2 percent for any product line. Where the billed quantity exceeds the lesser of the ordered quantity and the delivered quantity by more than 2 percent, the invoice line must be placed on hold and referred to a procurement reviewer. Partial deliveries are permitted; a supplier may invoice only for quantities that have been delivered.

## Duplicate Invoice Control

Two invoices carrying the same invoice number, amount, currency, invoice date and line items are treated as potential duplicates regardless of which supplier record submitted them. A potential duplicate must be held and reviewed before either invoice is paid. Recurring invoices for identical periodic orders are expected to carry distinct invoice numbers and dates and are not duplicates.

## Payout Account Changes and Shared Accounts

A bank account used by more than one supplier record requires review by the Vendor Master Data team, unless the account belongs to a registered payment intermediary such as an approved factoring agent. Reviewers should check whether the supplier records share ownership, addresses or contacts, and whether the records should be consolidated. Shared payout accounts are a review signal and are not, by themselves, evidence of wrongdoing.

## Segregation of Duties and Human Review

Automated controls may recommend that an invoice be held for review. Final decisions to confirm or dismiss an exception, and any decision to release or stop a payment, must be made by an authorised human reviewer and recorded with the reviewer's name, the decision and a justification.
